# VaPicha design refresh

Reference: https://html.awaikenthemes.com/fintech/index.html

The reference informed the dark hero, portrait-led composition, layered cards, generous typography, contrasting sections, scroll reveals and FAQ structure. Template source code, photographs, partner logos, reviews and performance claims were not copied.

Original VaPicha content, brand palette, logo, pricing and email-based demo flow are retained. The dashboard moved to a dedicated product showcase. New FAQ answers are based on the supplied company content.

## Original visual

Created using the built-in ImageGen tool. Saved to `public/lending-professional.png`. The subject is illustrative and is not presented as a customer or employee.

Prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium Nigerian lending software website hero photograph. Create an original editorial photograph of a confident Nigerian Black female financial professional in her early 30s with natural neatly styled hair, wearing a tasteful cream tailored blazer over a deep teal blouse, holding a thin tablet with both hands, smiling warmly and looking slightly toward camera. Waist-up portrait, person positioned slightly right of center, full head and hands visible, no cut-off elbows. Sophisticated softly blurred Lagos modern office with plants and warm daylight behind her, deep blue and muted teal architectural tones, no signage. Natural realistic skin texture, premium magazine lighting, candid approachable business photography, no exaggerated stock-photo grin. Portrait 4:5 composition, generous breathing room around her head. No text, numbers, logos, watermarks, graphics or UI. This is an illustrative brand photograph, not a real customer testimonial.

Animations respect reduced-motion preferences. FAQs use native details/summary controls, so they also work without JavaScript.

## Product-first revision

Following the user's correction, the dashboard is restored to the hero; its `vp` placeholder is replaced with the full VaPicha wordmark. The generated portrait is no longer used on the page.

Additional reference: https://budpay.com/ng — product-led hierarchy, dark grid backdrop, layered dashboard framing and illustrated product cards. No BudPay claims, certification badges, logos or assets were copied.

Visible motion includes an animated background grid, headline highlight, chart entrance/replay, floating status cards, a pulsing status dot, a four-stage interactive workflow with timed progress, and product hover effects. The hero's motion control pauses ambient animation and workflow autoplay. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. The chart remains selectable and the loan workflow can be explored manually.
